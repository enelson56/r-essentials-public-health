R Essentials for Public Health

An Applied Guide for Students

Author

Erik J. Nelson, PhD, MPH

Published

November 24, 2026

PUBLIC HEALTH · APPLIED R · HANDS-ON LEARNING

From Questions to Insight

Public health work begins with questions about people, communities, and the conditions that shape health. This book helps you build practical R skills to organize data, explore patterns, and communicate what you find through reproducible examples and hands-on practice.

Welcome to R Essentials for Public Health!

This online textbook provides a practical, hands-on introduction to using R for data management, analysis, and visualization. This is years of work being collated into one space for easier student access. I hope that this resource serves you well and somehow pays forward the mentoring I have been fortunate to receive.

About the Author

My name is Erik Nelson and I am a public health researcher. I have had academic stops at the University of Minnesota-Twin Cities, Saint Louis University, Indiana University-Bloomington, and now at Brigham Young University. I have been fortunate to work with many talented students and colleagues on a variety of public health research projects.

My research combines epidemiologic, statistical, and geospatial modeling techniques to identify where and why health disparities occur so that appropriate interventions can be developed to help overcome them. My research utilizes novel online recruitment techniques and Bayesian spatio-temporal models. My main areas of interest include health disparities, sexual health, screening, cancer prevention, environmental lead exposure, and social epidemiology. I have published over 70 peer-reviewed journal articles on a variety of public health issues such as human papillomavirus (HPV), HPV vaccination, lead exposure, violence, substance abuse, air pollution, and cognitive functioning.

Why I wrote this book

My purpose and philosophy behind this book

I have benefited from many open-source helpers over the years as I have learned to navigate R and RStudio. It seemed like it was time for me to put together all of my thoughts and helpers into one space for the next generation. The hope is that I will help you avoid the many mistakes I have made while showing you that you can do this too!

My intention is to use this as a teaching resource for my own students, but I hope it can be useful to others as well. I have tried to make this book as accessible as possible, and I welcome feedback on how to improve it. If you have any questions or suggestions, please feel free to reach out to me at erik_nelson@byu.edu.

How to Use This Book

The chapters combine guided instruction, reproducible code examples, and applied learning activities. The hope is you can identify patterns, learn the basics, and get to where you can communicate with artificial intelligence (AI) to speed up your processes. The intention is that you first go through the friction of learning the R coding language, and then unleash the power of AI help AFTER you have put in the effort. This will allow you to unlock the capabilities of R.

Each chapter contains:

  • Learning objectives
  • Explanations and examples
  • Guided lecture tasks
  • Opportunities for independent practice
  • Many chapters also have “optional exercises” to reinforce the material covered in class and let you practice on your own
